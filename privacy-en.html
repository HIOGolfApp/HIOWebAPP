HIO Golf Privacy Policy

Published: 24 July 2026 · Effective: 24 July 2026 · Version 1.0

HIO Golf ("the App") is a golf GPS rangefinder and scorecard tool. This policy explains, in plain language: what personal information we collect, why, where it is stored, who we share it with, and how you can access, correct or delete it — or close your account entirely.

Please read this policy before using the App. By using the App, you confirm that you have read and agree to it.

Passwords: the App uses one-time codes and third-party sign-in only. We do not set, collect or store any password. Session refresh tokens are stored as irreversible hashes; the original values are never written to our database.

2.2 Rounds and scores

  • Round records: the course and tee you selected, tee-off and finish times, and the game format (e.g. match play, four-ball).
  • Hole-by-hole scores: strokes, putts, penalties, fairway hit, green in regulation, and any note you write for a hole.
  • Round posts: the caption you write and the photos you choose to upload.
  • Weather snapshot: weather, temperature, wind direction and strength at the course when you finish. This is fetched using the course's location, not yours.

2.3 Social and user-generated content

  • Friends: friend requests you send or accept, and your friend list.
  • Group rounds: rounds you host or join, room invite codes, playing partners and their live scores.
  • Course reviews: your star rating and review text (reviews are publicly visible).
  • Feedback: anything you submit through the in-app feedback form.

2.4 Device and log data

  • Push token: if you allow notifications, we store the Apple-issued device token and its environment so we can deliver friend requests and round invitations. It is deleted when you turn notifications off or close your account.
  • IP address: used for two purposes. First, recorded temporarily when you request a sign-in code, purely for rate limiting, in an in-memory cache that expires within minutes. Second, as required by China's Provisions on the Administration of Internet User Account Information, we resolve and display the IP-derived region of each post at the time it is published (province level within mainland China, country level elsewhere) and the region of your most recent activity on your profile page; to do this we store your most recent IP, its region, and the region attached to each post. The IP address itself is never shown to other users; the region label is a regulatory requirement and cannot be turned off. Beyond these uses, IP data is not used for profiling.
  • Server access logs: standard web logs (timestamp, request path, status code) used for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.
  • Activity counter: we record whether you used the App on a given day, to compute aggregate daily/weekly active-user numbers. It is not used to build a profile and is never shared.

3. What we do not do

  • We integrate no third-party advertising, analytics or behavioural-tracking SDKs whatsoever.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, do not use the IDFA, and do not collect or share any advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information, and do not use it for marketing unrelated to the App.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, call history, clipboard, or list of installed apps.
  • We do not collect location in the background and do not keep any record of your movements.
  • We collect no biometric data of any kind.

5. Location data

  • Foreground only. We request "While Using the App" location only — never "Always". Once you leave the App, we stop.
  • Not stored. Your coordinates are used for that one distance calculation or nearby-course lookup and then discarded. Our database holds no user coordinates or location history.
  • Limited disclosure. If you look for nearby courses and our own course database has no match, your coordinates are sent to the AMap open platform to run a proximity search (see section 6). Your location is not shared with anyone else.

7. Storage, international transfers and retention

Storage. Our servers and database are located in mainland China (Tencent Cloud).

International transfers. Two features are provided by Apple and involve transferring data to Apple Inc. outside mainland China: Sign in with Apple (to verify your credential) and push notifications (your device token and notification content pass through APNs). If you prefer to avoid these transfers, use a different sign-in method and turn notifications off.

Retention. We keep your information while your account is active. After you delete your account we erase or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, except where law requires us to retain certain logs. Verification codes and rate-limiting records expire automatically within minutes.

8. Security

  • All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / 1.3).
  • We store no passwords; session tokens are stored hashed.
  • Access to servers and databases is restricted, and secrets are never distributed with the app binary.
  • Please note: photos you upload are stored as the original file on object storage. Their URLs are random and not enumerable, but anyone who obtains a URL can view the image. Photo files may also contain metadata such as capture time, device and location. If you would rather not retain that, strip it before uploading or avoid uploading sensitive photos.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights, including account deletion

  • Access and correction: view and edit your nickname, photo, gender, date of birth, handicap and home course under "Profile".
  • Delete individual content: delete any round, round photo, course review or feedback item, and remove friends, at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke location, camera, photo or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account: go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the App. This permanently deletes your account together with your sign-in identifiers, profile, rounds and scores, uploaded photos, friendships, reviews, feedback and push token. It cannot be undone.
  • Other rights: you may request a copy of your data, ask us to restrict processing, or ask us to explain our handling rules.

If you cannot complete any of the above in the App, email contact@hiogolf.cn. We will verify your identity and respond within 15 working days.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult golfers. We do not direct our services to children under 14 and do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are under 18, please read this policy together with a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without the required consent, we will delete it promptly.
