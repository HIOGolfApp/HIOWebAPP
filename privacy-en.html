HIO Golf Privacy Policy

Published: 24 July 2026 · Updated: 8 October 2026 · Effective: 8 October 2026 · Version 1.3

HIO Golf (一杆高尔夫) is a golf GPS rangefinder, scorecard and golf-meetup community, made up of the iOS app, the WeChat Mini Program "1杆高尔夫" and the hiogolf.cn website (together, "the Service"). This policy explains, in plain language: what personal information we collect, why, where it is stored, who we share it with, and how you can access, correct or delete it — or close your account entirely.

Please read this policy before using the Service. By using the Service, you confirm that you have read and agree to it.

2.2 Rounds and scores

  • Round records: the course, tee and nine-hole combination you selected, tee-off and finish times, and the game format (e.g. stroke play, match play, Nassau, 8-4-2-1, team play).
  • Hole-by-hole scores: strokes, putts, chips, penalties, tee-shot result, strokes taken to reach the green, fairway hit, green in regulation, and any note you write for a hole.
  • Guest scores: the nickname and scores you enter for an unregistered player (a "guest") in your group. They are used only to score that round and are not linked to any account.
  • Round posts: the caption, topics and photos you choose to add, and whether you mark a round as "not counted towards handicap".
  • Statistics and handicap: we calculate score statistics and a system-estimated handicap from your completed rounds, for your trend charts and your profile.
  • Weather snapshot: weather, temperature, wind direction and strength at the course when you finish. This is fetched using the course's location, not yours.

Purpose: to generate your scorecard, statistics, system-estimated handicap and share images; and, once you choose to publish, to show the round (including the hole-by-hole scorecard, caption and photos) in public feeds such as Recommended, Nearby, topic, course and profile feeds — signed-out visitors can also browse the Recommended feed. A round saved with "save score only, don't post" is not shown to others and does not appear in the course footprint others can see, but it is still counted in the summary statistics on your profile (such as rounds played and system-estimated handicap). Your score statistics (such as rounds played, system-estimated handicap, best score, average score and putts, fairway and green-in-regulation percentages) and your course footprint are shown to other users on your profile. In a group round, the players in your group can see your live scores.

2.3 Social and user-generated content

  • Follows and friends: your following and follower relationships (following each other makes you friends) and their counts, shown on your profile; and friend requests you send or accept.
  • Interactions: posts, topic and course tags, likes and comments you publish. Posts and comments appear in public feeds; only you can see who liked your posts.
  • Meetups: the meetups you post (type, course, time, group size, handicap limit, topic tags), applications and approvals, invitations, and the meetup scoreboard. Meetup posts appear publicly in the Meetups feed, and a meetup link you share can be opened by people who are not signed in.
  • Direct and group messages: the content (text and images) and timestamps of direct messages and meetup group chats (meetup members join the group chat automatically). Messages are stored on our servers encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); to deliver messages, filter prohibited content and handle reports, our servers are able to decrypt message content (this is not end-to-end encryption) — please do not send card numbers, passwords or similar sensitive data in chat. You can recall a message within 2 minutes of sending it, or delete individual messages or whole conversations from your own chat history; reported messages are submitted with necessary context for review.
  • Group rounds: rounds you host or join, round share codes and invite links, playing partners and their live scores. A round link you share can be previewed by people who are not signed in (course, host's nickname and profile photo, number of players).
  • Events and tournaments: events you create or register for (name, cover image, description, course, time, public or private), registration approvals, groupings, caddie assignments and hole-by-hole scores. A public event's details, player list, groupings and leaderboard are visible to everyone, including signed-out visitors; a private event is visible only to its host, players and caddies.
  • Caddies / scorers: records of you joining an event as a caddie or scorer through a check-in link or code, the players you are assigned to, and the scores you enter on their behalf.
  • Points: records of points you earn (for example by inviting friends), your available and lifetime points, and your tier.
  • Course reviews: your star rating and review text (reviews are publicly visible).
  • Mapping requests: the course information submitted when you tap "request mapping" on a course page.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you file (the reported content and reason) and your block list, used for content moderation and interaction isolation.
  • Content-safety records: snapshots of content that was reported or flagged as possibly violating by our content-safety services (text snapshots are stored encrypted), the detection result, the action taken and any account suspension, used for platform governance and appeals.

2.4 Device and log data

  • Push token (iOS app only): if you allow notifications, we store the Apple-issued device token and its environment so we can deliver friend requests, round and meetup invitations, interactions and messages. It is deleted when you turn notifications off or close your account. The Mini Program does not send you push notifications.
  • IP address: used for two purposes. First, recorded temporarily when you request a sign-in code, purely for rate limiting, in a cache that expires within a short time. Second, as required by China's Provisions on the Administration of Internet User Account Information, we resolve and display the IP-derived region of each post at the time it is published (province level within mainland China, country level elsewhere) and the region of your most recent activity on your profile page; to do this we store your most recent IP, its region, and the region attached to each post. The IP address itself is never shown to other users; the region label is a regulatory requirement and cannot be turned off. Beyond these uses, IP data is not used for profiling.
  • Server logs: like any online service, our servers keep access logs (time, IP address, requested URL, status code, referring page and client identifier; the requested URL may include request parameters such as location coordinates) and application logs, used for troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Access logs are rotated daily and deleted automatically after about 10 days.
  • Invite attribution: when you open our website through a friend's invite link, we record the IP address, operating-system version and screen size of that visit. After you create a new account in the iOS app, the app sends us your OS version and screen size (together with the request's IP address) so we can match them against invite-link visits from the previous 48 hours; the iOS app may also detect an invite link on the clipboard (see section 3). In the Mini Program, when you arrive through a card shared by a friend, we read the invite code carried in that card. This data is used solely to associate a newly registered account with the inviter (so both sides receive invite points) and to prevent abuse of invite rewards; it serves no other purpose and is no longer used once the attribution window has passed.
  • Activity counter: we record whether you used the Service on a given day, to compute aggregate daily/weekly active-user numbers. It is not used to build a profile and is never shared.

3. What we do not do

To be clear, the Service:

  • Integrates no third-party advertising, analytics or behavioural-tracking SDKs (no Umeng, Firebase, Sentry, AppsFlyer or anything similar).
  • Does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the IDFA, and does not collect or share any advertising identifier.
  • Does not sell or rent your personal information, and does not use it for marketing unrelated to the Service.
  • Does not personalise recommendations: the Recommended feed shows public content from everyone in order of posting time, and the Nearby feed filters only by city. We do not profile you or push content based on your personal characteristics.
  • Does not access your contacts, messages, call history or list of installed apps. The clipboard is read in one case only: within the first 7 days after you create an account, if the iOS app detects a web address on your clipboard, it reads the clipboard after iOS shows its "Allow Paste" prompt and you agree. It looks for a HIO Golf invite link on your device and sends us only the invite code it finds; nothing else on the clipboard is uploaded. The Mini Program never reads the clipboard; it only writes to it when you tap "Copy".
  • Does not collect location in the background, and does not keep your coordinates or movement history in our application database (for the short-term retention of server access logs, see section 5).
  • Collects no biometric data of any kind (face, fingerprint, voiceprint).

5. Location data

Location is sensitive personal information, so we strictly limit how we use it. Please read this section carefully:

  • Only while in use. The iOS app requests "While Using the App" location only — never "Always". The Mini Program reads your location only while it is in the foreground and does not request background location. Once you leave, we stop.
  • Not written to our application database. Your coordinates are used only for that distance calculation or nearby-course sorting and are not written to our application database. Our server access logs record the requested URL (which may include those coordinates) for troubleshooting and are deleted automatically after about 10 days. The city for the Nearby feed is matched from your location on your device, and only the city name is sent to us.
  • Limited disclosure. If you look for nearby courses and our own course database has no match, your coordinates may be sent to the AMap open platform to run a proximity search (see section 6). Your location is not shared with anyone else.

7. Storage, international transfers and retention

Storage

Our servers and database are located in mainland China (Tencent Cloud).

International transfers

Two features, used only in the iOS app, are provided by Apple and involve transferring data to Apple Inc. outside mainland China:

  • Sign in with Apple: to verify your credential;
  • Push notifications (APNs): your device token and notification content pass through Apple's push service to reach your device.

If you prefer to avoid these transfers, use a different sign-in method and turn notifications off. The Mini Program involves neither transfer.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active so that we can keep providing the Service. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information as described in section 9. Server access logs are deleted automatically after about 10 days; content-safety records and reports are kept for as long as required by law or as necessary for platform governance and appeals; verification codes, rate-limiting records and similar temporary data expire automatically within minutes; copies in database backups disappear as the backups rotate.

8. Security

  • All traffic between the clients and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / 1.3).
  • We store no passwords; session tokens are stored hashed; direct messages, group chats and the text snapshots in content-safety records are stored encrypted.
  • Access to servers and databases follows the principle of least privilege, and secrets are never distributed with the code.
  • Please note: images you upload (profile photos, post and round photos, chat images and event covers) are stored on object storage. Their URLs are random and not enumerable, but anyone who obtains a URL can view the image. Our clients re-compress images before uploading, which normally drops metadata such as capture time and location, but we cannot guarantee this on every device and version. Please do not upload photos that contain sensitive information.
  • No system is perfectly secure. We do our best to protect your information, and if a personal data breach occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities in a timely manner as required by law.

9. Your rights, including account deletion

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile photo, nickname, bio, gender, date of birth and handicap under Me → Edit Profile in the app or Mini Program, and link a phone number under Me → Settings.
  • Delete individual content: at any time you can delete your rounds and round photos, posts, comments and course reviews; delete messages and conversations from your chat history; withdraw a meetup application or leave an event; and unfollow or unblock users.
  • Block and report: block any user to isolate their interactions with you, and report violating content or users; we will act on reports in accordance with law and our terms.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off location, camera, photo, notification and similar access at any time in iOS Settings or, for the Mini Program, via "···" in the top-right corner → Settings.
  • Delete your account: go to Me → Settings → Delete Account in the app or Mini Program and confirm with a verification code sent to your registered phone number or email. If your account has no phone number or email linked (for example, you only use Sign in with Apple), email contact@hiogolf.cn to request deletion. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone:
    • Deleted: your sign-in identifiers (phone number, email, Apple identifier, WeChat OpenID / UnionID), your profile (nickname, bio, gender, date of birth, handicap, HIO ID), IP and region records, rounds you recorded on your own, your profile photo and round photos, posts and meetup posts you published (including their photos), likes and comments you made, course reviews, follow and friend relationships, your block list, meetup applications, your push token, your points and points records, and your activity records;
    • Kept in anonymised form: group rounds and events that you took part in or created and that involve other players (including scores, player lists and event details), and direct and group messages you sent (including images), remain in the other participants' records but are shown as "已注销球友" (deleted player) and are no longer linked to you; mapping requests you submitted are unlinked from your account;
    • Kept as required by law: content-safety records and reports are kept for the period described in section 7; copies in database backups disappear as the backups rotate.
  • Other rights: you may request a copy of your personal information, ask us to restrict processing, or ask us to explain this policy and our handling rules.

If you cannot complete any of the above in the app or Mini Program, or wish to exercise other rights, email contact@hiogolf.cn. After verifying your identity we will handle your request and reply, usually within 3 working days and in no case later than 15 working days.

10. Children

The Service is intended mainly for adult golfers and is not directed to children under 14. If you are under 14, please do not sign up on your own; if you really need to use the Service, your parent or guardian should read and agree to this policy, and you should use the Service under their guidance. If you are 14 or older but under 18, please read this policy and use the Service together with a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected a child's personal information without prior guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as possible. Parents and guardians can contact us as described in section 12.
